OPENGL VIEWPORT TEST

To test the OpenGL viewport, double-click:
  Start-OpenGL-4x.bat

Do not launch MaterialPainter.exe directly for this test.
The launcher sets WGPU_BACKEND=gl before starting the application.

To launch the Vulkan backend instead, use:
  Start-Vulkan-4x.bat

After opening a project, use the diagnostic snapshot and verify:
  backend: GL
(or the OpenGL backend name reported by wgpu)

If the diagnostic reports Vulkan, the OpenGL test was not launched correctly.
